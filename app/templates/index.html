How it works

From a quick check-in to a clearer view.

MindTrack fits into a few quiet minutes. Start small, and let the bigger picture build over time.

  1. Check inPick the mood that fits today and any feelings that came with it.
  2. Write it downA sentence or a page. Whatever feels right is enough.
  3. ReflectAnswer a gentle prompt that helps you look at things from another angle.
  4. Look backExplore trends and themes, and export a PDF summary if you'd like to share it with a counselor.
